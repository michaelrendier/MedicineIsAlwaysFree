# Olive Oil Infusion Protocol — 72h Low-Temperature Double Boiler

## Objective
Produce CBN-infused First Cold Pressed Extra Virgin Olive Oil (FCCPEVOO)
for transdermal delivery via bath bomb.

## Why FCCPEVOO?

FCCPEVOO has **lower cannabinoid binding affinity** than MCT oil or coconut oil.
This is intentional — lower binding affinity means:
- Cannabinoids are held loosely in the oil matrix
- At the skin-water interface (during the bath), cannabinoids **release readily**
- The oil layer on the bath water acts as a **slow-release transdermal reservoir**

FCCPEVOO also contains:
- Oleocanthal: natural COX inhibitor (ibuprofen-equivalent anti-inflammatory)
- Oleuropein: antioxidant — synergises with CBN anti-inflammatory action
- Polyphenols: may provide additional anti-neuroinflammatory benefit in MS

## Protocol

### Equipment
- Double boiler (two pots, one inside the other with water in the outer)
- Thermometer
- Glass container with lid
- Fine mesh strainer or cheesecloth

### Materials
- FCCPEVOO (cold-pressed, unfiltered preferred)
- CBN-dominant cannabis material (see CBN degeneration protocol)
- Ratio: 7 g cannabis material per 240 mL (1 cup) olive oil

### Steps

1. **Setup**
   - Fill outer pot with water
   - Place inner pot with olive oil on top
   - Target temperature: **65-70°C** at the oil surface (NOT boiling)
   - This is slightly above body temperature — preserves polyphenols in EVOO

2. **72-hour infusion**
   - Add cannabis material to oil
   - Maintain 65-70°C for 72 hours (LOWEST stable temperature)
   - Stir every 4-6 hours
   - Top up water in outer pot as it evaporates
   - Keep covered to prevent oxidation

3. **Extended time rationale**
   - Longer infusion → continued THCA/CBD → CBN conversion
   - Target: maximum CBN at the expense of THC/CBD
   - 72 hours at 65-70°C gives approximately 60-80% CBN conversion

4. **Strain**
   - Filter through cheesecloth
   - Press to extract all oil from plant material
   - Discard plant material

5. **Final product**
   - Deep green to olive-coloured infused oil
   - Store sealed at room temperature (shelf stable 6 months)
   - Refrigeration not needed (EVOO is stable at room temp)

## Bath Bomb Formulation

### Standard Batch (12 bath bombs)

**Base recipe per bomb:**
- 120g baking soda
- 60g citric acid
- 30g cornstarch
- 20g Epsom salt
- 5g FCCPEVOO (infused)
- 3g witch hazel (spray binder)

**CBN oil addition:**
- **1/4 teaspoon (≈1.25 mL) infused olive oil per 12 bombs**
- This is approximately **0.1 mL per bomb** = **micro-dose per bath**

The micro-thin oil layer on the bath water surface is the delivery mechanism.
One bath bomb per bath session.

## Bath Administration

### The Hydrodipping Process
1. Dissolve bath bomb in warm bath water (38-40°C — slightly above body temp)
2. Observe: a thin iridescent oil layer forms on the water surface
3. **Enter the bath slowly** — the oil layer wraps around the body as it submerges
4. The oil contacts all body surface simultaneously (the hydrodipping effect)
5. Remain in bath for 20-30 minutes
6. The body heat (37°C) promotes transdermal absorption
7. Do NOT shower immediately after — allow oil to remain on skin

### The Halocline Physics
The bath has two density layers:
- Oil layer (ρ ≈ 910 kg/m³) — floats on top
- Water (ρ ≈ 1000 kg/m³) — below

The patient's body immersed in water with oil on the surface = the body surface
is the **oil-water interface** — the halocline. Transdermal absorption occurs
at this σ=½ boundary.
