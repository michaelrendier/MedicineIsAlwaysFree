# CBN Production Protocol — Full Degeneration of THCA/CBDA

## Objective
Produce CBN-dominant extract for chemotherapy support via full thermal degeneration
of THCA and CBDA.

## Visual Indicator
**Forest green colour = correct product.**
- Fresh cannabis resin: amber/gold (THCA dominant)
- Partially decarboxylated: dark amber (THC/CBD dominant)
- **Fully degenerated: deep forest green (CBN dominant)**

The forest green colour arises from gravity separation:
the resinous CBN-rich fraction settles away from chlorophyll-containing plant matter.
The 'dirty water' (chlorophyll fraction) is discarded. The settled resin is the product.

## Chemistry

```
THCA  →[heat/time]→  THC   →[heat/oxidation]→  CBN
CBDA  →[heat/time]→  CBD   →[heat/oxidation]→  CBN
```

CBN (Cannabinol):
- MW: 310.43 g/mol
- Log P: 6.3 (highly lipophilic)
- Melting point: 76-77°C
- Boiling point: 185°C
- UV absorption: 230, 285 nm

## Protocol

### Starting Material
- High-THCA or high-CBDA cannabis flower, kief, or hash
- Forest green starting material is already CBN-rich

### Equipment
- Double boiler or water bath
- Thermometer (infrared or probe)
- Food-grade container (glass preferred)
- Fine mesh strainer

### Steps

1. **Gravity separation of hash (optional — for kief/hash starting material)**
   - Place material in cold water (4°C)
   - Allow to settle 30 minutes
   - Decant 'dirty water' (contains chlorophyll)
   - Collect settled resin
   - Dry at room temperature

2. **Extended low-temperature decarboxylation**
   - Temperature: 100-110°C (do NOT exceed 120°C — volatilises CBN)
   - Time: 4-6 hours minimum
   - Stir every 30 minutes
   - The colour shift from amber to forest green indicates THCA→CBN completion

3. **Optional: ethanol extraction**
   - Cold wash with food-grade ethanol at -20°C
   - Removes residual waxes and chlorophyll
   - Evaporate ethanol at <40°C
   - Result: CBN-rich oil, deep green to black colour

### Quality Check
- Forest green colour: confirmed CBN (visual)
- Taste: slightly bitter, less harsh than THC oil
- HPLC confirmation (if available): target >80% CBN in final product

## Administration

### Chemotherapy Support Formulation
- Dose: approximately 0.5 mL CBN oil (concentration dependent)
- Carrier: 0.5 teaspoon food-grade ethyl alcohol (Everclear 190-proof)
- Dilutant: 200 mL orange juice (or equivalent acidic juice)
- Mix: add alcohol to OJ, add CBN oil dropwise, stir

### Expected Effects
1. Appetite stimulation within 2-4 hours
2. Deep sleep onset within 4-6 hours
3. Sleep duration: 18-26 hours
4. Patient wakes to urinate (hydration need)
5. **Rehydration protocol:** electrolyte solution immediately on waking;
   encourage water intake before returning to sleep

### Contraindications
- Not for use with CNS depressants without medical supervision
- Avoid in pregnancy
- Check for CYP3A4 interactions (CBN is a CYP3A4 inhibitor)
