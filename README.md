### 1. Chemotherapy Support — CBN via Full Degeneration

**The formulation:** Full degeneration of THCA and CBDA into CBN (cannabinol).

**How CBN is produced:**
- THCA and CBDA are the raw, acidic forms in the fresh plant
- Heat or prolonged aging decarboxylates THCA → THC, CBDA → CBD
- Extended heat/oxidation continues: THC → CBN, CBD → CBN
- CBN is the terminal degradation product — the "forest green" stage
- **Forest green colour** is the visual indicator: no chlorophyll because gravity
  separation of hash from 'dirty' water removes the green plant matter.
  The hash settles clean. The water carries the chlorophyll away.
  What remains is CBN-dominant resin.

**The preparation:**
- Half teaspoon ethyl alcohol (food grade, e.g. Everclear) into a glass of orange
  juice or other dilutant
- The alcohol acts as a carrier solvent to suspend the CBN oil in the juice
- CBN is highly lipophilic (log P ≈ 6.3) — it needs a carrier

**Effect profile:**
- **Appetite stimulation** — critical for chemotherapy patients who cannot eat
- **Sleep induction** — 22+ hours of sleep; the body uses this for repair
- **Rehydration requirement** — patient wakes to urinate after ~22 hours;
  rehydration with electrolytes is essential before returning to sleep

### 2. Multiple Sclerosis — Bath Bomb CBN Olive Oil Treatment

**The formulation:** Phytocannabinoid-infused First Cold Pressed Extra Virgin Olive Oil
in a generic bath bomb carrier.

**The oil preparation:**
- 72-hour double boiler cook at LOWEST possible temperature
- Carrier: First Cold Pressed Extra Virgin Olive Oil (FCCPEVOO)
  - FCCPEVOO does not like holding cannabinoids — this is a feature, not a bug
  - The lower binding affinity means the cannabinoids stay available at the surface
  - Extended low heat drives higher CBN concentration (the strong pain relief form)
- The long cook time + low heat = maximum CBN conversion from THC/CBD

**The bath bomb delivery:**
- 1/4 teaspoon of the infused oil into 12 standard bath bombs
  (approximately 0.02 mL per bomb)
- Bath bombs dissolve: the olive oil creates a **micro-thin surface layer**
  on the bath water
- The patient enters the bath: **hydrodipping**
  - The oil layer wraps intimately around the entire body surface
  - A continuous, micro-thin film of CBN-rich oil contacts every cm² of skin
  - Transdermal absorption across the full body surface simultaneously

**The case:**

> and walked without pain for the first time.

- Single treatment session
- Full body immersion in CBN-olive oil bath
- Effect onset: during the bath (~20 minutes)
- Duration: several hours post-bath
- No adverse effects reported

**The mechanism:**
- Transdermal CBN absorption bypasses hepatic first-pass metabolism
- Full-body application = massive surface area → significant systemic load
- CBN is strongly analgesic (CB2 agonist, also TRPV1 modulation)
- In MS: the demyelination creates inflammatory J_B (Blue) imbalance

- The olive oil carrier: low-affinity binding = the oil releases the CBN at the
